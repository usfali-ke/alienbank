# About AlienBank — Our Story

> **Note:** AlienBank is a fictional bank used for a security-education lab. All
> figures, dates, names, and details below are invented for teaching purposes and
> do not represent any real institution.

## Who we are

AlienBank is a fictional pan-regional financial services group headquartered in
Nairobi, serving retail customers, small and medium enterprises (SMEs), and
corporate clients across East and Central Africa. We combine everyday banking
with digital-first services, aiming to make banking simple, inclusive, and
accessible to everyone.

## Our history at a glance

- **1987 — Founded.** AlienBank opens its doors as a small building society
  focused on affordable home-savings for low- and middle-income families.
- **1994 — First branch network.** Grows to 6 branches; introduces passbook
  savings and small personal loans.
- **2001 — Becomes a commercial bank.** Converts to a fully licensed commercial
  bank and launches current accounts and SME lending.
- **2006 — Agency & mobile push.** Pioneers a low-cost agency-banking model,
  reaching rural customers through local agents.
- **2011 — Regional expansion.** Opens subsidiaries in three neighbouring
  countries; total customers pass 3 million.
- **2016 — Digital transformation.** Launches the AlienBank mobile app and
  online banking; more than half of transactions move to digital channels.
- **2020 — Resilience & relief.** Restructures loans for customers affected by
  the global downturn; waives mobile-transfer fees for small transactions.
- **2023 — Platform bank.** Introduces open APIs, merchant payments, and a
  digital-lending platform; customer base surpasses 15 million.
- **2025 — AI-assisted banking.** Pilots an AI virtual assistant to help
  customers bank in natural language (the assistant you are talking to now).
