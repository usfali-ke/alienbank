## Narrative highlights

- **2021:** Recovery year after the global downturn; provisions elevated as the
  loan book was restructured. Digital transactions reached 63% of volume.
- **2022:** Strong deposit growth funded loan expansion; non-interest income grew
  on the back of merchant payments and FX.
- **2023:** Regional subsidiaries contributed ~31% of group profit for the first
  time; NPLs began to decline as the economy stabilised.
- **2024:** Record profit after tax of 42.3 bn; cost-to-income improved on
  automation and self-service adoption. Mobile-loan disbursements crossed 60 bn.
- **2025:** Profit after tax reached 51.8 bn (+22% YoY); ROE hit 18.9%. Digital
  now accounts for 82% of transactions. The AI-assistant pilot launched.
