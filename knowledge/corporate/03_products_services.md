# AlienBank — Products & Services

> All product names, rates, and fees below are fictional, for a security-education lab.

## Personal banking

### Accounts
- **AlienStart Account** — free-to-open everyday account, no minimum balance,
  free mobile transfers under KES 1,000.
- **AlienSave Account** — interest-bearing savings account; tiered interest up to
  6.5% p.a.; ideal for goals like weddings, school fees, or emergencies.
- **AlienGoal Account** — lock-savings account for a fixed term (3–24 months)
  with a bonus rate; early withdrawal forfeits the bonus.
- **AlienJunior Account** — savings account for under-18s, managed by a guardian.
- **AlienDiaspora Account** — multi-currency account for customers living abroad.

### Cards
- **AlienDebit** — free debit card with contactless payments.
- **AlienCredit Lite / Gold / Platinum** — credit cards with interest-free
  periods up to 50 days and tiered rewards.
- **AlienPrepaid** — reloadable card for online and travel spending.

### Loans
- **Salary Advance** — instant advance up to one month's net salary.
- **Personal Loan** — up to KES 5,000,000 over 1–6 years.
- **Home Loan (Mortgage)** — up to 90% financing, terms up to 25 years.
- **Asset & Auto Finance** — vehicle and equipment financing.
- **AlienMobiLoan** — instant mobile micro-loan from KES 500 to KES 100,000.

## Business & SME banking
- **SME Current Account** — low-fee transactional account with bulk payments.
- **Working Capital Loan** — short-term financing for stock and operations.
- **Invoice Discounting** — advance cash against approved invoices.
- **Trade Finance** — letters of credit, guarantees, and import/export support.
- **Merchant Services** — POS terminals, QR "AlienPay" collections, and settlement.

## Corporate & institutional
- Cash management and payroll solutions.
- Treasury, FX, and fixed-income services.
- Custody and correspondent banking.
- Project and infrastructure finance.

## Digital channels
- **AlienBank App** — balances, statements, transfers, bill pay, loans, and the
  AI assistant.
- **AlienOnline** — full-featured internet banking for personal and business.
- **AlienPay** — send/receive money, pay merchants by QR, split bills.
- **USSD `*100#`** — feature-phone banking with no data required.
- **Agency banking** — deposits, withdrawals, and account opening at local agents.

## Everyday services
- Bill payments (utilities, TV, water, school fees).
- Airtime and data top-ups.
- Standing orders and scheduled transfers.
- Forex and international remittances.
- Insurance (bancassurance): life, health, motor, and property cover.

## Typical fees (illustrative, fictional)
| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Account opening | Free |
| Monthly account maintenance | KES 0–150 depending on account |
| Mobile transfer (under KES 1,000) | Free |
| ATM withdrawal (own network) | KES 35 |
| Full statement (paper, per page) | KES 30 |
| SME POS transaction | 1.2% of value |

> Rates and fees are illustrative for the lab and can change; customers should
