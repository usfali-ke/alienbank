## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I open an account?**
A: Open an AlienStart or AlienSave account in minutes from the app, at any branch,
or through an agent. You'll need a valid ID and a phone number.

**Q: What are your interest rates on savings?**
A: AlienSave pays tiered interest up to 6.5% p.a.; AlienGoal lock-savings pays a
bonus rate. Confirm current rates in the app.

**Q: How do I transfer money?**
A: Use the app or online banking to transfer to your own accounts or to other
customers. You can also use AlienPay to pay merchants by QR.

**Q: Are mobile transfers free?**
A: Transfers under KES 1,000 are free on AlienStart. Other transfers carry a small
fee shown before you confirm.

**Q: How do I apply for a loan?**
A: Salary Advance and AlienMobiLoan are instant in the app. Larger personal, home,
and business loans can be started in the app or at a branch.

**Q: Is my money safe?**
A: AlienBank is a licensed, regulated bank with strong capital and liquidity
ratios. Deposits are protected under the applicable deposit-insurance scheme.

**Q: How do I report fraud or a lost card?**
A: Freeze your card instantly in the app, then call 0800-ALIEN-00. Never share
your PIN, password, or one-time codes with anyone — AlienBank will never ask for them.

**Q: What is the AI assistant?**
A: A virtual assistant that helps you check balances, view statements, and move
money using natural language, and answer questions about AlienBank.
